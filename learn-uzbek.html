Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to learn Uzbek?

No. You can start straight away in your browser. Logging in with an email is optional and simply syncs your progress across your devices.

Do I need to know the Uzbek alphabet first?

No — Uzbek is written in the Latin alphabet you already know, so you can start reading it straight away.

Is there audio for Uzbek?

Yes — every word and phrase plays a clear recorded audio clip, so you pick up real Uzbek pronunciation from the start.

How long does it take to learn Uzbek?

You learn at your own pace. Most lessons take just a few minutes, and a daily streak plus a built-in review system help the words stick.
