First words

The handful you will use every day

The o in these spellings is a short aw: nomoshkar sounds closer to nawmawshkar. In Kolkata you address people by kinship — dada দাদা to a man, didi দিদি to a woman.

dhonnobadthank youধন্যবাদ

hyanyesহ্যাঁ
nanoনা
thik achheOK, that is fineঠিক আছে
achchhaI see / rightআচ্ছা
ashigoodbyeআসি
dadabrother — to a manদাদা
didisister — to a womanদিদি

Being polite

Bengali has two words for you

Use apni আপনি with anyone you have just met. Its verbs end in -en: kemon achhen. tumi তুমি is for friends and children.
apniyou (polite)আপনি
tumiyou (friendly)তুমি
kemon achhenhow are you?কেমন আছেন?
bhalo achhiI am fineভালো আছি
apni kemon achhenand how are you?আপনি কেমন আছেন?
ashunplease come inআসুন
boshunplease sitবসুন
bolungo ahead, tell meবলুন

Meeting someone

Names, countries, first contact

There is no verb to be in the present — amar nam John is literally my name John. amra at jon is there are eight of us; any number fits that gap.
amar nammy name is…আমার নাম
apnar nam kiwhat is your name?আপনার নাম কী?
apnar desh kothaywhere are you from?আপনার দেশ কোথায়?
bangla shikhchhiI am learning Banglaবাংলা শিখছি
ektu bangla bolte pariI can speak a little Banglaএকটু বাংলা বলতে পারি
amraweআমরা
amra at jonthere are eight of usআমরা আট জন
berate eshechhiI have come to visitবেড়াতে এসেছি

Numbers, money & time

Prices, counting and the clock

Money is taka টাকা. Learn the tens — prices are usually ponchash 50, shottor 70, dersho 150. kal means both tomorrow and yesterday; the verb tells you which.

Getting around

Taxis, autos and the metro

Ask the fare before you get in: bhara koto?. Yellow taxis should run the meter — mitare jaben?. Autos run fixed shared routes, so just name the destination.
jabenwill you go?যাবেন?
bhara kotohow much is the fare?ভাড়া কত?
mitare jabenwill you go by meter?মিটারে যাবেন?
taxitaxiট্যাক্সি
autoauto-rickshawঅটো
metrothe metroমেট্রো
basbusবাস
ekhane namboI will get off hereএখানে নামব
thamunplease stopথামুন
kokhon chharbewhen does it leave?কখন ছাড়বে?

Eating & drinking

Tea, rice, fish and the bill

Being a guest

You will be fed, and then fed again

You will be offered more food several times. arektu? a little more is the question; pet bhore gechhe I am full is the answer. khub bhalo hoyechhe compliments the cooking.
arektua little more?আরেকটু?
ar na dhonnobadno more, thank youআর না, ধন্যবাদ
pet bhore gechheI am fullপেট ভরে গেছে
khub bhalo hoyechheit was really goodখুব ভালো হয়েছে
darunwonderfulদারুণ
onek dhonnobadthank you very muchঅনেক ধন্যবাদ
kheye janstay and eatখেয়ে যান
abar ashbencome againআবার আসবেন

Shopping & bargaining

Asking the price and pushing back

In a market the first price is an opening offer. khub dami too expensive, then kom korun lower it, is most of the negotiation.

neboI will take itনেব

dokanshopদোকান
byasthat is allব্যাস
