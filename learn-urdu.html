Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to learn Urdu?

No. You can start straight away in your browser. Logging in with an email is optional and simply syncs your progress across your devices.

Do I need to know the Urdu alphabet first?

No. The course starts by teaching the Urdu (Nastaliq) script one letter at a time, with audio, before building into words and sentences.

Is there audio for Urdu?

Yes — every word and phrase plays a clear recorded audio clip, so you pick up real Urdu pronunciation from the start.

How long does it take to learn Urdu?

You learn at your own pace. Most lessons take just a few minutes, and a daily streak plus a built-in review system help the words stick.
