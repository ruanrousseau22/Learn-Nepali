About the course content

Bhasaly's courses are researched carefully against textbooks, dictionaries and published course materials, but they are not a substitute for a teacher, and they carry no guarantee of accuracy. Languages vary by region, register and community, and a phrase that is correct in one place can be wrong or rude in another. Do not rely on Bhasaly alone where a mistranslation would matter — medical, legal, safety or immigration situations especially.

If you spot an error, please report it: Settings → Report an error or suggestion, or the link at the end of any lesson. Corrections from speakers are genuinely welcome and are how the courses improve.

Religious studies

Bhasaly includes an optional Religious studies mode with Christian Bible stories retold in each language. It is clearly separated from the language courses, it is never mixed into them, and it is entirely optional — nothing in the courses requires you to open it. The retellings are paraphrases written for learners, not a Bible translation, and should not be quoted as scripture.
