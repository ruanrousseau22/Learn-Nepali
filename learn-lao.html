About the course

Bhasaly's Lao course takes you from your very first words to everyday conversation across 12 levels — 74 topics and 370 short, tap-based lessons (no typing required). You will learn the Lao script one letter at a time. Along the way you will pick up essential greetings, numbers, and useful phrases, each with a recorded audio clip. It runs in any browser on phone or computer, and your progress saves automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to learn Lao?

No. You can start straight away in your browser. Logging in with an email is optional and simply syncs your progress across your devices.

Do I need to know the Lao alphabet first?

No. The course starts by teaching the Lao script one letter at a time, with audio, before building into words and sentences.

Is there audio for Lao?

Yes — every word and phrase plays a clear recorded audio clip, so you pick up real Lao pronunciation from the start.

How long does it take to learn Lao?

You learn at your own pace. Most lessons take just a few minutes, and a daily streak plus a built-in review system help the words stick.
