Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to learn Javanese?

No. You can start straight away in your browser. Logging in with an email is optional and simply syncs your progress across your devices.

Do I need to know the Javanese alphabet first?

No — Javanese is written in the Latin alphabet you already know, so you can start reading it straight away.

Is there audio for Javanese?

Yes — every word and phrase plays a clear recorded audio clip, so you pick up real Javanese pronunciation from the start.

How long does it take to learn Javanese?

You learn at your own pace. Most lessons take just a few minutes, and a daily streak plus a built-in review system help the words stick.
