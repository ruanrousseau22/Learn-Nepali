About the course

From your first letters to everyday conversation, Bhasaly teaches Burmese one short, tap-based lesson at a time — written in the Burmese script. Every word carries recorded audio, and you learn on any phone or computer.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to learn Burmese?

No. You can start straight away in your browser. Logging in with an email is optional and simply syncs your progress across your devices.

Do I need to know the Burmese alphabet first?

No. The course starts by teaching the Burmese script one letter at a time, with audio, before building into words and sentences.

Is there audio for Burmese?

Yes — every word and phrase plays a clear recorded audio clip, so you pick up real Burmese pronunciation from the start.

How long does it take to learn Burmese?

You learn at your own pace. Most lessons take just a few minutes, and a daily streak plus a built-in review system help the words stick.
