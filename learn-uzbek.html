About the course

Bhasaly's free Uzbek course takes you from your very first words to everyday conversation across 12 levels — 84 topics and 420 short, tap-based lessons (no typing required). It is written in the Latin alphabet, so it is easy to start reading. Along the way you will pick up essential greetings, numbers, and useful phrases, each with a recorded audio clip.
