First words

jee keeps it respectful

The reply to assalaam alaikum is the same greeting turned around: wa alaikum assalaam. jee before haan or nahin keeps every yes and no respectful; koi baat nahin forgives everything.
assalaam alaikumhelloالسلام علیکم
wa alaikum assalaamhello (the reply)وعلیکم السلام
shukriyathank youشکریہ
khuda haafizgoodbyeخدا حافظ

jee nahinnoجی نہیں
maaf keejiyesorry · excuse meمعاف کیجیے
theek haiOK, fineٹھیک ہے
koi baat nahinno problemکوئی بات نہیں

Being polite

Strangers are family

bhai and baaji address your own age, uncle and aunty the generation above. saahab after a name — Khan saahab — shows respect; the polite aap does the rest. Everyday please is simply the English word — baraa-e mehrbaani belongs to signs and announcements.
bhaaibrother — a man your ageبھائی
baajisister — a woman your ageباجی
uncleuncle — an older manانکل
auntyaunty — an older womanآنٹی
saahabsir (after a name)صاحب
baraa-e mehrbaaniplease (formal / signs)برائے مہربانی

Meeting someone

Introductions

meraa naam … hai — my name is …. kyaa haal hai follows any hello, and aahista boliye — speak slowly — rescues you when the answers come too fast.

kyaa haal haihow are you?کیا حال ہے

Welcomes & refills

tashreef laaiye ushers you in; aur leejiye means your plate is being refilled. suniye — listen — is how anyone catches anyone’s attention.

Mehmaan-nawaazi

Hospitality is the pride

mehmaan-nawaazi — hospitality — is Pakistan’s pride, and visitors get the full treatment. Chai appears unasked; pet bhar gayaa with bahut mazedaar ends seconds with honour intact.
mehmaanguestمہمان
pet bhar gayaaI am fullپیٹ بھر گیا
bahut mazedaarvery tastyبہت مزیدار
basenough (said smiling)بس

Eating

Chai, roti and the heat

The bazaar

The dance has steps

bahut mehngaa! then kam keejiye is the dance; aakhri qeemat — final price — is the seller’s last word. Fixed-price shops say so and mean it.

Getting help

Serious words, hopefully unused

madad keejiye — please help — is the sentence to know; say it clearly. tabiyat theek nahin tells a chemist everything — pharmacies dispense advice as freely as pills.

Staying in touch

Phone, wifi & photos
