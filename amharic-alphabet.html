Common questions

How many letters are in the Amharic alphabet?

This chart lists 7 vowels and 33 consonants — 40 letters in total — plus the ten numerals.

Can I hear how each Amharic letter sounds?

Yes. Open the alphabet in Bhasaly and tap any letter to play a recorded clip of its sound. No account is needed.

What is the best way to learn the Ethiopic (fidel) script?

One letter at a time, with sound, then straight into real words. Bhasaly's Amharic course starts with the script and builds from there.
