About the course

Bhasaly's free Nepali course takes you from your very first words to everyday conversation across 12 levels — 77 topics and 385 short, tap-based lessons (no typing required). You will learn the Devanagari script one letter at a time. Along the way you will pick up essential greetings, numbers, and useful phrases, each with a recorded audio clip. It runs in any browser on phone or computer, and your progress saves automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Nepali course on Bhasaly free?

Yes. Every lesson across all 12 levels is completely free, with no ads and no paywall.

Do I need to know the Nepali alphabet first?

No. The course starts by teaching the Devanagari script one letter at a time, with audio, before building into words and sentences.

Is there audio for Nepali?

Yes — every word and phrase plays a clear recorded audio clip, so you pick up real Nepali pronunciation from the start.

How long does it take to learn Nepali?

You learn at your own pace. Most lessons take just a few minutes, and a daily streak plus a built-in review system help the words stick.
