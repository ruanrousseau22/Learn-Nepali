First words

Courtesy is krama on purpose

The courtesy set is krama by design — matur nuwun has no casual substitute and everyone says it. ya wis closes anything agreeably; sugeng tindak is the goodbye said to the person leaving.

Monggo

Java in one word

monggo is please, go ahead, after you — said with a slight bow as you pass anyone, anywhere. mas and mbak address your own generation kindly.

Getting around

Becak, ojek, sepur

The becak is pedal-powered and slow — agree the price before you climb in. ojek motorbikes run on apps now. The sepur — the train — is the best way across Java, so book ahead.

The warung

Where Java eats
