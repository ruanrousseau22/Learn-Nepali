About the course

Bhasaly's free Khmer course takes you from your very first words to everyday conversation across 12 levels — 84 topics and 420 short, tap-based lessons (no typing required). You will learn the Khmer script one letter at a time. Along the way you will pick up essential greetings, numbers, and useful phrases, each with a recorded audio clip. It runs in any browser on phone or computer, and your progress saves automatically.
