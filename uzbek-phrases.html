The ones you use every hour

Being polite

aka, opa and the two pleases

Address any man aka, any woman opa — warmth, not familiarity. marhamat offers — help yourself — and iltimos asks. The reply to katta rahmat is arzimaydi, not at all.

Eating

Osh, non and the refills

Being a guest

The dasturxon rules

The dasturxon fills the moment you sit. Choy is poured a third full — that is respect, not thrift: a brimming cup hints the guest should leave. toʻydim ends seconds politely.

Do I need an account to use these Uzbek phrases?

Do I need to read the Uzbek script to use these?

No. Every line is romanized first, so you can say it straight away. The script is there alongside if you want to start reading it.
