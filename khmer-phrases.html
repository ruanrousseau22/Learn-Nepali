First words

baat for men, chaah for women

Polite yes depends on YOUR gender: men say baat, women chaah. chumreap suor comes with palms together — the sompeah; suosdey is the everyday hello.
suosdeyhelloសួស្តី
chumreap suorhello (formal, with the sompeah)ជំរាបសួរ
arkunthank youអរគុណ
som tohsorry · excuse meសុំទោស
baatyes (men say)បាទ
chaahyes (women say)ចាស
tenoទេ
lea haeybyeលាហើយ

Being polite

Everyone is family

Address anyone slightly older as bong; your parents’ generation are pu and ming. arkun bong sounds warmer than arkun alone; min ei te smooths everything over.
bongolder brother/sister — anyone a bit olderបង
puuncle — an older manពូ
mingaunt — an older womanមីង
sompleaseសូម
arkun bongthank you (to someone older)អរគុណបង
min ei teno problem · it is okayមិនអីទេ

What you will hear

Greetings that sound like questions

tov na — where are you going — and nham bay haey rue nov — eaten yet? — are greetings, not interrogations. A vague cheerful answer is the right answer: nham haey.
tov nawhere are you going? (a greeting)ទៅណា
nham bay haey rue noveaten yet? (a greeting)ញ៉ាំបាយហើយឬនៅ
nham haeyeaten already (the answer)ញ៉ាំហើយ
maen haeythat is right!មែនហើយ
som choulplease come inសូមចូល

Dollars & riel

Two currencies at once

Cambodia runs on both at once: prices in dollars, change in riel at 4,000 to the dollar. muoy poan riel — 1,000r — is a quarter; keep small notes for tuk-tuks.
muoy1មួយ
pram5ប្រាំ
dop10ដប់
muoy roy100មួយរយ
poan1,000ពាន់
muoy poan riel1,000 riel (≈ 25 cents)មួយពាន់រៀល
pram poan riel5,000 rielប្រាំពាន់រៀល
muoy meun riel10,000 rielមួយមុឺនរៀល
dolladollarដុល្លារ
rielrielរៀល
do luyto change moneyដូរលុយ

Tuk-tuks

Agree the price first

Settle the fare before you climb in — tov phsar ponman? starts it. A few thousand riel crosses most towns; moto-taxis are half the price.

Eating

Rice early, coffee iced

bay is rice and the meal itself — lunch runs early, by eleven. kafe tuk kak — iced coffee — is the national drink, and num pang baguettes are the French inheritance.

At the phsar

The friendly game

Bargaining is a smiling sport at the market. thaok cheang nih mean te? asks for cheaper; ot mean luy te — no money on me — waves off persistent sellers kindly.
phsarthe marketផ្សារ
thlai pektoo expensive!ថ្លៃពេក
thaokcheapថោក
thaok cheang nih mean teanything cheaper?ថោកជាងនេះមានទេ
luymoneyលុយ
ot mean luy teno money on meអត់មានលុយទេ

Staying somewhere

Rooms & cool air

In this heat the question is the fan or the aircon, not hot water. saat — clean — is worth confirming with a look at the room first.

Getting help

Serious words, hopefully unused

chuoy khnhom phang! — please help me — is the sentence to know; say it clearly. hav polih calls the police; carry your guesthouse card for the ride home.

thnammedicineថ្នាំ
