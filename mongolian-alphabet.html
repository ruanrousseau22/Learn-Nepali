Common questions

How many letters are in the Mongolian alphabet?

This chart lists 13 vowels and 22 consonants — 35 letters in total — plus the ten numerals.

Can I hear how each Mongolian letter sounds?

Yes. Open the alphabet in Bhasaly and tap any letter to play a recorded clip of its sound. No account is needed.

What is the best way to learn the Mongolian Cyrillic alphabet?

One letter at a time, with sound, then straight into real words. Bhasaly's Mongolian course starts with the script and builds from there.
