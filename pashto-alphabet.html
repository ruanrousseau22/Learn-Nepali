Common questions

How many letters are in the Pashto alphabet?

This chart lists 8 vowels and 36 consonants — 44 letters in total — plus the ten numerals.

Can I hear how each Pashto letter sounds?

Yes. Open the alphabet in Bhasaly and tap any letter to play a recorded clip of its sound. No account is needed.

What is the best way to learn the Pashto (Perso-Arabic) script?

One letter at a time, with sound, then straight into real words. Bhasaly's Pashto course starts with the script and builds from there.
