First words

Hello, thanks & no problem

sabaidee works from dawn to midnight. baw pen nyang — no problem — answers thanks, apologies and mishaps alike.
sabaideehelloສະບາຍດີ
khop jaithank youຂອບໃຈ
baw pen nyangno problem · you are welcomeບໍ່ເປັນຫຍັງ
kho thotsorry · excuse meຂໍໂທດ
maenyes (it is)ແມ່ນ
baw maenno (it is not)ບໍ່ແມ່ນ
jaoyes (polite) — also: youເຈົ້າ
laa kawngoodbyeລາກ່ອນ
phop kan maisee you againພົບກັນໃໝ່

Being polite

Soft words, soft country

Requests end in dae and invitations start with sern. Nothing here is urgent — matching the pace is the politest thing a visitor can do.
khomay I have … (starts a request)ຂໍ
dae…please (ends a request)ແດ່
kalunaaplease (formal)ກະລຸນາ
sernplease (inviting)ເຊີນ
nyin diiglad · with pleasureຍິນດີ
phoenoughພໍ

Meeting someone

Introductions

khoi seu … — my name is …. huu noi neung — I know a little — is the honest, welcome answer about your Lao.

vao kheunsay it againເວົ້າຄືນ

maa jaak(I) come from …ມາຈາກ

What you will hear

Greetings & invitations

pai sai? — where are you off to — and kin khao laew baw? — eaten yet — are greetings, not questions. Answer vaguely, smile, walk on.

maa lincome visit!ມາຫຼິ້ນ
sok diigood luck!ໂຊກດີ

Numbers

Kip come in thousands

Everything is thousands of kip: water around haa phan, noodle soup meun and up, a guesthouse in saen. The banknotes carry the same numbers, which makes paying easy.

Getting around

Agree the price, enjoy the pace

Tuk-tuks and songthaews run on agreed prices — settle before you sit. jot yuu nii stops the driver where you need.
lot tuk tuktuk-tukລົດຕຸກຕຸກ
lot mebusລົດເມ
piiticketປີ້
jot yuu niistop hereຈອດຢູ່ນີ້
yutstop!ຢຸດ

Sticky rice

The national staple

khao niao comes in a lidded basket: roll a ball with your RIGHT hand, dip, eat — never put rice back. saep! is the compliment every cook wants.

Shopping

Gentle bargaining

Morning markets are the real Laos. phaeng phot opens the negotiation; it stays gentle — a few thousand kip either way, never a battle.

phaeng phottoo expensiveແພງໂພດ

Getting help

Serious words, hopefully unused

suay duay! is the urgent cry for help; suay dae is the calm request. lavang — careful — is what friends will call after you.

tamluatpoliceຕຳຫຼວດ
hong mawhospitalໂຮງໝໍ
mawdoctorໝໍ
yaamedicineຢາ
lavangbe careful!ລະວັງ

Staying in touch

Phone, wifi & photos

Guesthouse wifi is common but slow. SIMs are cheap with a passport. thaai huup dai baw? asks before the camera comes out — always ask in villages.

Common questions

How do you say hello in Lao?

Sabaidee. The greetings section above has the full set, including the reply you are likely to hear back.

Do I need an account to use these Lao phrases?

No. Every phrase on this page is open to read, and you can hear all of them in Bhasaly without signing up.

Do I need to read the Lao script to use these?

No. Every line is romanized first, so you can say it straight away. The script is there alongside if you want to start reading it.
