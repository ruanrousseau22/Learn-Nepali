Common questions

How many letters are in the Bengali alphabet?

This chart lists 11 vowels and 39 consonants — 50 letters in total — plus the ten numerals.

Can I hear how each Bengali letter sounds?

Yes. Open the alphabet in Bhasaly and tap any letter to play a recorded clip of its sound. No account is needed.

What is the best way to learn the Bengali script?

One letter at a time, with sound, then straight into real words. Bhasaly's Bengali course starts with the script and builds from there.
