Advice for Intensive

Start with the script. Spend your first two weeks on it — Nepali reads almost exactly as it sounds, so once the alphabet clicks you can sound out nearly any word. Practice the characters anytime on the Alphabet page, and the first unit on the Intensive path covers the script too.

Then lean on these videos, conversation practice and tools.
